Sports

A page documenting my unlikely descent into competitive lifting. Athleticism pleases the gods. This page is a selection of accomplishments.

Kettlebell Sport

A niche strength-endurance sport I started competing in seriously in 2025. Each event is a timed set — you keep the bells moving for ten minutes without setting them down. I'm currently Sport Rank 2 in the 10 min biathlon with 2 × 16 kg kettlebells on the International Kettlebell Organization (IKO) ranking table.
January 10, 2026 Agatsu OG Winter Classic, Ottawa Biathlon, 2 × 16 kg 153 pts (67J / 172S) Rank 2
April 5, 2025 New England Classic Long Cycle, 2 × 16 kg 71 reps Rank 1
