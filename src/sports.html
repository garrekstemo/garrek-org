Sports

A page documenting my unlikely descent into competitive lifting. Athleticism pleases the gods. This page is a selection of accomplishments.

Kettlebell Sport

A niche strength-endurance sport I started competing in seriously in 2025. Each event is a timed set — you keep the bells moving for ten minutes without setting them down. On the International Kettlebell Organization (IKO) ranking table I'm currently Sport Rank 1 in long cycle and Sport Rank 2 in biathlon.

The events:

  • Long Cycle — clean two kettlebells to the rack, then jerk them overhead, repeated for the whole set.
  • Biathlon — two lifts scored together: the jerk (two kettlebells) followed by the snatch (one kettlebell, switching hands once). Snatch reps count half.
  • Double Half Snatch — the snatch, but with two kettlebells, racking them between reps.
